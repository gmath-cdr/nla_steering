What we actually changed

The output shift did not come from editing the test prompt. A direction was built offline, then added to the model's hidden state at every prompt and decoding position. The prompt, seed, decoding settings, and score were held constant within each sweep.

NLA = Natural Language Autoencoder. AV verbalizes an activation into text; AR reconstructs text back into a residual-space activation.

Authored AR text contrast

Write a positive and negative description, reconstruct each into the residual space, and subtract:

d = AR(+) - AR(-)

AV-edit-AR

Verbalize one real layer-20 state, edit that exact prose, reconstruct edited and original versions, then subtract them.

J-derived arm

Start from a completed NLA parent, approximate it with up to 25 J-lens token atoms, then steer with the approximation or its parent-minus-approximation remainder.

Open-ended result 2 - a failed probe became a content-matched success

The first "one-word failure" was mostly the wrong target

Four wave-1 edits inserted excited, enthusiastic, cheerful, or expressive. They were scored for emoji use even though they did not name emoji. Those directions were not generally inactive: several changed exclamation and excitement behavior instead.

What changed after review

  • Target words were made explicit.
  • Every parent arm was rerun with fresh seeds.
  • k25 J-atom approximations replaced the historical top-50 proxy.
  • Emoji flooding was marked as degeneration.

What this rules out

It rules out the simple conclusion that "single-site AV-edit-AR directions are dead." It does not isolate edit size from wording, tokenization, edit location, or effective dose.

Lesson. A score can declare a direction a failure when the direction changed a different behavior than the chosen probe. Target wording, readout content, and outcome metric must be aligned before interpreting a null.

J-space, exactly - diagnostic plus derived intervention

We steered NLA parents, then their J-derived surrogates

Precise answer. The J-lens was first used to read NLA directions as ranked token alignments. We then used its token-direction dictionary to build a sparse k25 approximation of an NLA parent and replaced the parent in the additive steering hook with that approximation or with the parent-minus-approximation remainder. This is more than measuring alignment, but it is not pure direct steering with an independently selected J token atom.

Sparse k25 fitnonnegative combination c of at most 25 atoms

Separate steering armsunit(d), unit(c), unit(d-c)

The Qwen layer-20 J-lens was averaged over 160 frozen WikiText-103 passages; its 80/80 split-half matrix cosine was 0.943 (a stability check, not causal validation). This is first-order residual-space alignment, not ordinary token co-occurrence counting or a live activation transplant.

What was causal

The k25 approximation and remainder were each injected and generated new outputs. They were not only plotted or decoded.

What was normalized

Parent, approximation, and remainder were independently unit-normalized. Their curves compare potency per unit norm.

What remains untested

No archived arm injects one independently chosen J-lens atom as the root direction. A draft harness specifies that direct test, but it is not frozen or completed.

Key causal limit. Independent normalization prevents a native-magnitude mediation claim. A potent unit approximation does not prove that it carries most of the original vector's natural causal contribution, and an active remainder is not evidence that it lies outside all J-readable directions.

J-space result - content helped; scalar ranking did not

The k25 share did not predict which direction would steer

The visually readable token content often matched the observed behavior, but the size of the recovered k25 piece was not a success score. High-share failures and lower-share successes overlap.

Design limits

  • One model and one residual layer.
  • Only 1-5 prompt blocks per family.
  • Mostly one random direction per comparison.
  • Best alpha often selected on the same sweep.
  • Two file pairs are exact replays; alpha-zero replies repeat more broadly.
  • Prompt/seed replies are repeated measures, not independent evidence units.
  • All-position injection differs from paper-style single-position tests.

Concise conclusion. The 69-file Qwen package shows that NLA AR contrasts and AV-edit-AR directions can causally steer open-ended style and broad semantic content. J-lens token readouts are promising diagnostics, and J-derived k25 surrogates can be potent, but neither scalar J metrics nor the component/remainder split provides a universal success or localization rule. The clean next mechanism test is direct J-atom versus NLA-parent steering on held-out prompts with frozen alpha selection.
